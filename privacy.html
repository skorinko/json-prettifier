Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

In short: JSON Prettifier does not collect, share or sell any personal data. It reads a page only to show it formatted, on your own device, and stores only its settings.

Who we are

JSON Prettifier for Safari is published by Dmitry Skorinko, an individual developer, through the App Store.

What the extension sees

By default, JSON Prettifier has no access to your websites. Safari lets it into a page only when you click its toolbar button, choose it in the page menu, press its keyboard shortcut or use its context menu, and only into that page.

If you turn on automatic formatting, Safari asks you to give it access to websites. It then checks each page you open for one thing: whether Safari shows it as text that looks like JSON. Ordinary web pages are left as they are, and you can turn automatic formatting off, for one site or for all, at any time.

When it formats a page, it reads the page’s text where it is shown, in Safari on your device, to draw it as a tree. It doesn’t keep that text after you close the page, and has no access to your browsing history.

What is stored

Your settings, such as the color theme, the text size and the sites where automatic formatting is off, are kept in Safari’s storage for the extension, on your device. Text you send to the Scratchpad from a page is kept there only until the Scratchpad opens it. Removing the app removes everything it stored.

Network requests

JSON Prettifier makes no requests to the developer or to any third party. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code. Links inside a document open only when you click them.

Image previews are off unless you turn them on in the viewer’s settings. Then, when you point at or open an image address in a document, Safari loads that image from its address, as it loads any image on the web, without telling that site which page you came from. JSON Web Tokens are decoded on your device and never sent anywhere.

Purchases

JSON Prettifier is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and the developer does not receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Children

JSON Prettifier collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
